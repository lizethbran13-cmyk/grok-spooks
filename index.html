HOW TO PLAY

  1. Walk with the left stick. Your flashlight points where you walk.
  2. FLASH stuns ghosts. Then hold VAC to suck them in. Pull the stick away from the ghost to drain its HP faster. When the meter fills, tap SLAM!
  3. BLOW pushes crates, turns mirrors, spins pinwheels and fires back things you caught with VAC.
  4. PLUNGER yanks sunglasses, helmets and hats off, and pulls levers and crates.
  5. DARK light reveals invisible ghosts, hidden switches, secret doors and gems.
  6. VAC curtains, rugs, cobwebs and furniture to find coins, hearts and gems!
  7. Puzzles need two hunters. Play with a friend, or tap SWAP to make a green Goo clone and switch between you. If you swap while holding VAC, BLOW or DARK, the one you leave keeps doing it.
  8. Clear every ghost to turn the lights back on. Find the area key for the gold door to the boss. Beat the boss to unlock the next floor in the elevator.
  9. Spend coins on upgrades (pause menu). Candy the ghost dog barks near hidden gems and Boos.
  10. Got spooked? In co-op a friend revives you by standing next to you. Solo, you pop back at the room door. No game over!
